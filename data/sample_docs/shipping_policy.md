# Lumen Home Shipping Policy

## Where we ship

We currently ship to Italy, Germany, France, Spain, Austria, the Netherlands, Belgium, and Portugal. We do not ship outside these countries.

## Processing time

Orders are prepared within 1 to 2 business days. You receive an email with a tracking link as soon as your order leaves our warehouse.

## Shipping options

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs 4.95 euros. Standard shipping is free for orders above 60 euros.

Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 12.95 euros. Express orders placed before 14:00 CET on a business day leave the warehouse the same day.

## Furniture delivery

Furniture is delivered by a specialized partner within 7 to 14 business days. The partner contacts you to schedule a delivery date. Furniture is delivered to the ground floor entrance of your building. Furniture cannot be delivered to PO boxes or parcel lockers.

## Changing the delivery address

You can change the delivery address in your account until the order ships. After the order has shipped, the address can no longer be changed.

## Lost packages

If your tracking has not updated for 10 business days, contact support and we will open an investigation with the carrier. If the package is confirmed lost, we send a replacement or a full refund.
