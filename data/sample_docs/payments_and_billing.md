# Payments and Billing

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Klarna.

Klarna lets you pay in 3 interest free installments. It is available for orders between 50 and 1,500 euros.

## When you are charged

Your card or account is authorized when you place the order and charged when the order ships. If an order ships in several parcels, you are charged for each parcel when it ships.

## Prices and VAT

All prices on our website include VAT. Shipping costs are shown at checkout before you pay.

## Invoices

An invoice is available in your account within 24 hours after your order ships. Business customers can add a company name and VAT number at checkout to receive a business invoice. A business invoice cannot be issued after the order has been placed.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, your order is held for 48 hours so you can update your payment method. After 48 hours, the order is cancelled automatically.

## Discount codes

Only one discount code can be used per order. Discount codes cannot be used to buy gift cards.

## Price drop protection

If the price of an item you bought drops within 7 days of your purchase, we refund the difference. You can request this once per item by contacting support.
