# Account and Privacy

## Creating an account

An account is optional. You can check out as a guest, but an account lets you track orders, download invoices, and collect Lumen Circle points.

## Password reset

If you forget your password, request a reset link from the login page. The link is valid for 30 minutes. After that, you need to request a new one.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account under Settings, then Privacy. Deletion is completed within 30 days. Order and invoice records are kept for 10 years because tax law requires it, but they are no longer linked to an active account.

## Marketing emails

We only send marketing emails if you opt in. Every marketing email contains an unsubscribe link, and you can also turn them off under Settings, then Notifications. Order and shipping emails are always sent, because they are part of your purchase.

## How we use your data

We use your data to process orders, deliver products, and provide support. We never sell your personal data to third parties. Payment details are handled by our payment providers and are never stored on our servers.

## Privacy questions

For questions about your data, contact privacy@lumenhome.example. We reply within 5 business days.
