Refund Policy

Customers can return any product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include the receipt or order number. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.

Exceptions

Gift cards, downloadable software, and personalized items cannot be returned. Items marked as final sale on the product page are not eligible for a refund or exchange. Products damaged by misuse, accidents, or unauthorized repairs are also excluded.

Damaged or Wrong Items

If a product arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, contact support within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of the product and the packaging. We will send a replacement at no cost or issue a full refund, including the original shipping fee. You do not need to return a damaged item unless support asks for it.

Return Shipping

For standard returns, the customer pays the return shipping cost unless the order total was above 100 euros, in which case we provide a free prepaid return label. Exchanges for a different size or color are free once per order.
