# Frequently Asked Questions

## How can I contact support?

Live chat is available Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 18:00 CET. You can also email support@lumenhome.example, and we reply within 24 hours on business days. We do not offer phone support.

## Do you have physical stores?

No. Lumen Home is an online only store, and we do not have showrooms or physical shops.

## Can I cancel my order?

You can cancel an order from your account within 1 hour of placing it. After that, the order is already being prepared, so you need to wait for delivery and then return it.

## How long are gift cards valid?

Gift cards are valid for 3 years from the date of purchase. They cannot be exchanged for cash, and they cannot be used to buy other gift cards.

## Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes. Gift wrapping costs 3.50 euros per item and can be selected at checkout. Gift wrapped orders include a card with your message and no prices.

## Is your packaging sustainable?

Our boxes and paper fillers are made from 90 percent recycled material, and we do not use plastic air pillows.

## Can I order a sample of a fabric?

Yes. Fabric samples for curtains and upholstery cost 2 euros each, and the cost is refunded if you later order the full product.
