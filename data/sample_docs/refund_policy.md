# Lumen Home Refund Policy

## Returns

You can return most products within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and returned with the receipt or order number. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item.

Gold members of the Lumen Circle loyalty program have an extended return window of 60 days.

## Items that cannot be returned

Gift cards, personalized or engraved items, and items marked as final sale cannot be returned or exchanged. For hygiene reasons, pillows and duvets cannot be returned once their seal has been opened. Products damaged by misuse, accidents, or unauthorized repairs are also excluded.

## Damaged or wrong items

If a product arrives damaged, or you received the wrong item, contact support within 48 hours of delivery and include photos of the product and the packaging. We will send a free replacement or issue a full refund, including the original shipping fee. You do not need to send a damaged item back unless support asks for it.

## Return shipping costs

For standard returns, a return shipping fee of 6.95 euros is deducted from your refund. If your order total was above 100 euros, we provide a free prepaid return label instead. Exchanges for a different size or color are free once per order.

## Furniture returns

Furniture is collected from your home by our delivery partner. A pickup fee of 39 euros applies to furniture returns, unless the item arrived damaged.
